# dbt Exercise Labs: Customer Revenue & Product Performance

Hands-on analytics engineering exercises using dbt, SQL, DuckDB, and a CSV seed dataset.

## Project Overview

This repository contains two beginner-friendly dbt labs focused on data transformation, aggregation, reusable model dependencies, and data validation.

### Lab 1 — Customer Revenue Analytics

**Model:** `customer_revenue_analytics`

Calculates customer-level business metrics:
- `customer`: Customer name
- `total_orders`: Number of distinct orders
- `total_sales`: Total sales amount
- `avg_order_value`: Average order value

Results:

| Customer | Total Orders | Total Sales | Average Order Value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Alice | 2 | 77500 | 38750 |
| Bob | 2 | 66200 | 33100 |
| Charlie | 1 | 15000 | 15000 |

### Lab 2 — Product Performance Dashboard

**Model:** `product_performance`

Summarizes product performance using:
- `product`: Product name
- `total_orders`: Number of distinct orders containing the product
- `product_revenue`: Total revenue generated by the product

Results:

| Product | Total Orders | Product Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Laptop | 1 | 75000 |
| Phone | 1 | 65000 |
| Monitor | 1 | 15000 |
| Keyboard | 1 | 2500 |
| Mouse | 1 | 1200 |
